The day draws nigh!  We gather strength.  Soon, we shall bring Vixies to the Realmz.  The great sacrifice must be made.  When enough power is attained, Vixies shall appear in the guise of a holy noble.  She shall be a worldly woman of great physical strength and power.Those who oppose the mighty Vixies shall be altered into spider form.   Doom will enshroud all puny kingdoms that dare to resist.The time draws near!  She has already sent her most trusted aid to begin the preparations for her arrival.  The "Widow of the Web"  demands the sacrifice of Vixies' most loyal priestess.Most honored of human disciples!  You shall be the vessel for my rulership of the Realmz.  You shall be sacrificed and sealed in the holy tomb with Vixies' spiders.  Once Vixies' spiders have consumed the flesh from your body, your skeletal figure shall be taken to the Widow of the Web.  Only then shall the ceremony take place to summon Vixies to the Realmz.Until the time draws near, the Widow of the Web shall await nearby in a secret cavern she has prepared for the ceremony.You shall live forever as a martyr to the cause of Vixies.<<< Click & Drag Mouse To Move About >>><<< Double Click To End This Message  >>>
