We hunt the wild antelope that range the sun-blessed fields and feast upon the berries and nuts of the moon-dappled forests. These are our lands, and forever shall be. There have been those who sought to take our lands from us. But they were crushed beneath our hooves and struck down by our blades. We survive while they are food for worms. It is only natural, for we are stronger and smarter than those we have faced.Õ The old centaur prances about as he speaks and occasionally rears his front hooves into the air.After a time he calms himself and begins to comb his flanks with a stiff brush. ÔBut we have not always fought alone. I remember when the Sidhe came to aid us in our time of need. The Shadowgaunt railed against us; giant rat faced creatures with claws that rend your flanks and shred your armour. Fast enough to pull your tail right from you before you could turn and place a hoof in their eye. Proud they were too, they would adorn themselves in the braided tails of our fallen and consided our powdered hooves a delicacy. But eventually they fell to the mighty charge of the Centaur and the deadly bows and magic of the Sidhe.Õ ÔMy great grandsire told me of the hated Boneshavers, a race of such vileness that they made the Shadowgaunt seem quite tame. It is their ruined city that lies to the east along the Barrier river. We hold to tradition and routinely patrol the blighted place, as it is said that one day great evil will once again take root there. In fact we have found and executed several suspicious humans attempting to sneak past our warders and enter the city. You were lucky you had the sense not to cross blades with a centaur or you would have joined them.Õ He strikes his hooves out rapidly and saunters about, a dangerous look crossing his face.ÔBut I forget my manners. You clearly are not like these misguided cretins. My scouts have brought word of your battle alongside the Ents and it is clear to me that such noble beings as they would not would suffer the company of knaves. You may therefore have free licence in the Centaur lands - but know that we are always watching and will not hesitate to strike against those who abuse our hospitality.Õ<<< Click & Drag Mouse To Move About >>><<< Double Click To End This Message  >>>
