Noone knew what they called themselves for they were not the most talkative of creatures. In fact their only recorded contact with others consisted of slaughter and atrocities best left forgotten. The name of their principal city, Yr Coreag, became associated with the darkest nightmares to make even the stoutest of our Riders quail. They practiced the cruelest of sorceries: shadow magic, blood magic and the construction of great structures and artifacts made of bones. It was these obscene structures which gave rise to their apt name ‘Boneshavers’. Riders had routed a host of them in battle only to take flight at the sight of what they swore were great pyramids of skulls. Rumours spread and the name stuck.To this day I am filled with rage and anguish at the memory of the first of their many victims. An oddly mishapen but proud people, known as the Fomorians, who were apparently of Gnomish stock, had the misfortune of residing too close to Yr Coreag. It was not before the Formors were the victims of wholesale slaughter that the other races thought to put down these vermin. To this day one rarely sees a Gnome and one can’t help but feel shame when one ambles by, a lost look upon their face. Finally an alliance of goodly races set out to defeat the Boneshavers, foremost among them were the Danaan. By this time the ‘Shavers evil and power had grown, fueled by their many victims. Dreadful sorceries were hurled against the alliance, shadow fiends and blood curses smote us, while the Boneshavers used cunning contraptions to crush our troops from afar. A great shadow mage among them, name of Clur'can, was individually responsible for the death of many of my dear comrades.But the worst was yet to come. One will never know who sought out who; which evil was attracted to the vile corruption of the other ... but sometime during this long and costly war the Grey God himself cast his hand into the fray. Then did we know despair.(the manuscript ends in a long tear ... leaving the end of this tale a mystery). <<< Click & Drag Mouse To Move About >>><<< Double Click To End This Message  >>>
