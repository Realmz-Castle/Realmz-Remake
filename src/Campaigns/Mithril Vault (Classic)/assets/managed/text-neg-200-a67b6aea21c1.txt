He grows weary of life in the Realmz.  His impatience can only mean ultimate pain and suffering for those of the Circle.It is imperative that you find out who is behind the theft of the Geyser Stone.  Even as I write this he has begun to devour the minds of more than a dozen slaves.  From what I can tell he will need the energy in order to extract the most hideous of tortures upon a fellow member of the Circle of Blood.  I can only pray that it's not I that has displeased him.For the love of all that is holy, find out what has become of the Geyser Stone.  My minions have captured and tortured a trio of Harpies.  Before their deaths they said something strange.  They kept mumbling about the dwarves and how they were attacking anything that came near the Mithril Mines with such ferocity that no creatures dared go near them.This is odd indeed for the Dwarven clans seem to live in relative peace with all.  It's my believe they may know something of the Geyser Stone.  They have a vast network of informers and seem to know something of everything that happens in the dark recesses of Silver Peak.Our need of information on the whereabouts of the Geyser Stone is so great that I fear you must risk using your natural talents to give us some clue.  The master grows ever so impatient and every member of the circle knows what that can mean.I only wish he would go back from whence he came.  Though I know not for certain where that may be I have heard that he hails from a body from beyond the stars.  How that can be I do not know but I quiver at the thought of the power he must have in order to travel the heavens as would a god.Mentat VolbiloveCircle of Blood7th Ring 4th Quadrant<<< Click & Drag Mouse To Move About >>><<< Double Click To End This Message  >>>
