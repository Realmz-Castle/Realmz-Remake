Our whole economy and welfare rests on the fair trade with the Dwarven clans to the east in the silver mountains.Mithril has always flowed from the mines and we have always been on good terms with the Dwarven leadership.  However, things have taken a drastic turn for the worse.It is common knowledge in the city of our trip to meet with the Dwarven king so I will touch on that briefly.  As you probably know, we met with the Dwarven king in order to find out why the flow of Mithril has stopped.  His answer did not sit well with us and was an obvious fabrication.  We even went as far as to cast a few magical spells and they confirmed that he was not telling us the whole truth.All further attempts to meet with the clan leaders has met with stiff resistance or outright refusal.What we need of you is for you to investigate the situation and find out the truth as to why the Mithril has stopped flowing.Already sign of decay have started to appear and people begin to whisper of leaving for greener pastures.To make matters worse, many of the races that live within Silver Peak are finding the Dwarves belligerent.  The Dwarven armies are attempting to keep any outsiders away from the mines.With the increased tension some of the creatures native to Silver Peak are finding it difficult to hunt and survive and have began to raid outlying farming communities.We must find out the truth behind this mysterious turn of events and set it right at any cost.<<< Click & Drag Mouse To Move About >>><<< Double Click To End This Message  >>>
