We have managed to locate the relic and now have it in our possession.I sent this communiqué ahead to inform  you of our impending arrival.  I would like to tell you how we came to be in possession of the relic as its true origins as best as I can guess.  This information may be of some interest to you.As you have foretold, the relic was indeed found in a very remote section of the underworld.  We battled many large beasts of a terrible nature to obtain the relic.In doing so we lost many good compatriots but that is of little concern to one such as you.It would seem that "The Geyser" is not a thing of nature or of any magic of the Realmz.  As far as I can tell, it's not even a thing of the Realmz at all.  I can only describe it as some form of strange device created by beings from another world beyond the stars.We tracked the device to a small round craft lodged in the side of an immense cavern.  About the craft were many creatures that I have never seen nor heard tales of before.It was a mighty battle but we managed to defeat the creatures.  A few of the beasts were humanoid in form but appeared to have been made from metal.  They carried strange weapons that shot beams of light.We lost nearly three full companies of our best men before we could overwhelm them.  We are bringing back some of these light weapons along with 'The Geyser' for you to examine.As per our agreement we were promised a large sum of wealth for the return of the relic and we would be pleased if you could have the payment ready when we arrive.Some of our men have been disappearing each time we make camp.  Something is following us and I would not be surprised if it was the original owner of the relic.  I am sure it would be no match for one of your powers but we seem to be nothing more than prey in its eyes.By the time this message reaches you we should be no more than a few days in reaching you.Your ServantCaptain Gail Wyrmrider12th Acid Claw Brigade<<< Click & Drag Mouse To Move About >>><<< Double Click To End This Message  >>>
