This letter you must keep in order to present it (along with the heads of your querries) upon receiving the pledged reward.In case of dire need, you may seek the advice of the Herzog of the Heartlands in Aranys. Her Excellency the Herzog is fully briefed and is playing a coordinating role on this matter, by the Writ of his Dread and Invincible Majesty, the Sublime King of Sharranth.Written on this day of Midweek, 13th of the month of Harvest, Year of the Great War {1166 R.R. (Realmz Reckoning)}Ertham ZohSupreme Warlock of His Dread and Invincible Majesty, the WarLord andSublime King of Sharranth.<<< Click & Drag Mouse To Move About >>><<< Double Click To End This Message  >>>
