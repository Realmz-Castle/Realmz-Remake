Although not our allies, we had numerous treaties of friendship and trade with the Wailsrondese, a proud, hard-working, honest and peaceful people whom we always respected and cared for. Therefore, it was with utter disbelief, bewilderment, and anger that we learned that Tanirith did nothing to help her ally and friend, despite being bound by a treaty of alliance to do so.In this respect, we consider that Tanirith bears the shame of Wailsrond's fall and destruction, almost as if Tanirith had commited such atrocity herself. And this shame does not only befall upon the Lord Paladine and the High Council, but upon the citizens of Tanirith as well.To this effect, it is our opinion that Tanirith is untrustworthy of being an ally and, sadly, we consider our alliance treaty with Tanirith null and void. However, for the sake of continued peaceful relations and commerce between our cities, we have not revoked our non-agression and trade treaties with Tanirith.Nonetheless, it is with a heavy heart indeed that we must caution the city of Tanirith, and its shameful leaders, that Lantharond will henceforth remain ever watchful, judging and mistrustful of Tanirith's actions, whether they be political, military or commercial in nature.Written on this day of Resting, 6th day of the month of Coldmantle, Year of the Great War {1166 R.R. (Realmz Reckoning)} Erion DragonshieldRoyal Regent of Their Sublime Excellencies, Queen Aramina Dragonclaw and King Vallar Dragonheart, Scions of Lantharond and Anointed Keepers of the Dragonthrone <<< Click & Drag Mouse To Move About >>><<< Double Click To End This Message  >>>
