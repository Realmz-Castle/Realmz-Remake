However, I must agree with our Herzog of the Heartlands that it would be wiser strategically to turn Aranys into the new center of the Heartlands, both military andcommercially. Remember that the ultimate prize of the present campaign is to acquire Tanirith. Hence, occupying Wailsrond would be costly in men and weapons, all the while weakening our hold on Sparklestone (and its riches) and leaving us on the defensive from attacks by Tanirith.So, it is my hope that you now see the wisdom in leveling Wailsrond instead of occupying it. In war and conquest, one must sacrifice his best piece at one time or another, if one is to win the contest. That is how I view Wailsrond: a strategic piece that is needed to be sacrificed if we are to succeed with our final push to Tanirith.So I urge you, as your Liege and long-time friend, to do as I asked of you, and level that city once and for all. Of course, I expect you to ransack it in order to obtain as much riches as you can, in order to provide us with more monies to finance our campaign.I wish you success and victory;Written on this day of Rising, 27th of the month of Sunning, Year of the Great War{1166 R.R. (Realmz Reckoning)}Anthraxus StormDread and Invincible Majesty, WarLord and Sublime King of Sharranth.<<< Click & Drag Mouse To Move About >>><<< Double Click To End This Message  >>>
