Even the Lord Paladine, the councilor Jarad Noll, and High Commander Fireblade are hard pressed and at a loss as to correct this current state of affairs.I can therefore assure you that the campaign of the Heartlands has nothing to fear from Tanirith, and that Wailsrond stands effectively alone against us.Be aware, however, that the Lord Paladine is seeking the help of adventurers, most likely those who defeated the Overlords the last time (which are known as the "Heroes of the Sword Lands"). If they have left the region, then the Lord Paladine will be willing to hire the help of any adventuring party. At least, that is what our ally reported, and this has been confirmed by my agents. In fact, the Lord Paladine intends to give command of a warband to such adventurers, thus circumventing the current impasse at the High Council concerning sending troops to help the Wailsrondese.However, whether such measures prove fruitful to Tanirith and Wailsrond will be up to our forces already established and entrenched in the Heartlands.Written on this day of Endweek, 11th day of the month of Harvest, Year of the Great War {1166 R.R. (Realmz Reckoning)} The RavenRoyal Spy of His Dread and Invincible Majesty, the Sublime Warlord and King of Sharranth <<< Click & Drag Mouse To Move About >>><<< Double Click To End This Message  >>>
