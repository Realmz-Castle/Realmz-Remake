Although it shames me to have learned of his involvement with those malevolent beings known asOverlords and their evil conspiracies, the responsabilities of managing my late father's estates and companies are now mine to bear. Of course, I was made aware upon my return of all these long business negociations between you and my father concerning the acquisition of your company by my father's. That is why I wish to continue in his name these negociations, in hoping that our partnership will finally take shape for our mutual interests. It is my hope that you feel the same; thus, I am at your disposition should you wish to resume the negociations, at your earliest convenience.Yours sincerely,Shalla ThrallWritten on this day of Setting, 9th day of the Month of Sunning, Year of the Great War {1166 R.R.}<<< Click & Drag Mouse To Move About >>><<< Double Click To End This Message  >>>
