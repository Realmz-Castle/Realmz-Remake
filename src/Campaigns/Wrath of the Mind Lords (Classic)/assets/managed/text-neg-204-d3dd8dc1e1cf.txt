Hear ye!His Royal Lordship, Baron Jund Holdar, Governor of Mountedge by writ of his Royal Majesty Endanuur II, is hereby promising the generous reward of ten thousand gold pieces -paid in gems and jewelries- to any brave and noble soul (or souls) valiant enough to rid the countryside (known also as the Wilder Lands) of orcs and highwaymen, including those conspiring agencies to whom they may be beholden. Items of value and of much magical dweomer may be added to the reward. Once anyone brings forth tangible proof of such valiant deed to Phantagolus, Royal Mage of Mountedge, then may the reward be deservingly given to whom brings such proof.Proclaimed and written on this day of Midweek, 21st of the month of Greening, 529 R.R.<<< Click & Drag Mouse To Move About >>><<< Double Click To End This Message  >>>
